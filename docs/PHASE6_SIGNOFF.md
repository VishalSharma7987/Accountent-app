# Phase 6 sign-off (period-end and final accounts)

Owner / accountant checklist for the end of Phase 6. The detailed workings and the tick sheets are in the acceptance pack `docs/test-packs/phase6-full-year-accounts-2026-27/` (WORKING.md, SIGNOFF.md, VERIFICATION_SHEET.xlsx). An unsigned pack is not a pass criterion.

## What the pack proves (three full-year dummy clients, every statement to the rupee)
1. Trading proprietor: adjustments (outstanding, prepaid, accrued, income in advance, bad debt, provision for doubtful debts), closing stock approval, fixed-asset register with Companies Act and income-tax depreciation (half rate for an addition under 180 days), Trading A/c, P&L, Balance Sheet (horizontal and vertical), Cash Flow, year-end close with carry-forward, reopen and close again.
2. Partnership 3:2: interest on capital (day-weighted) and on drawings, partner remuneration for part of a year, appropriation with the rounding paisa to the last partner, close and carry-forward, reopen.
3. Company: Schedule III Balance Sheet and Statement of Profit and Loss, 15 notes, previous-year column from the signed-off first year (closed in the pack), Cash Flow, and the refusal to reopen a year while the next year has postings.

## Sign-off questions (the accountant answers; readings of the tool)
Debtors base of the provision; interest on capital and drawings basis; remuneration for part of a year; rounding paisa in the profit share; Companies Act day count and tranche rule; income-tax half-rate counting; cash-flow classification; closing-stock source; drawings ledger identification; statements excluding the closing vouchers; one stock ledger; Schedule III classification. The full wording is in the pack's SIGNOFF.md.

## Pending legal values (nothing is verified; docs/PHASE6_LEGAL_VALUES.md section 5)
Income-tax block rates and half-rate rule under the Income-tax Act 2025 (and the 1961 Act rows), Schedule II useful lives and the 5 percent residual, Schedule III line items and the group defaults. They stay `pending` and print on every depreciation working and export until the CA confirms them from the Act.
